Selected work

Devices and methods, each started from a clinical need in a specific setting.

Where
Johns Hopkins and Sight Design Surgical, Baltimore
When
2021 to present
Stage
Prototype

SILC, a hand-held cataract fragmentation device

Manual small-incision cataract surgery carries much of the world's cataract volume. SILC breaks up and removes the cataract through an incision of about 3 mm, with no ultrasound energy and no electricity, so it fits the existing workflow and its cost.
