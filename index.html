Where

Stage
Prototype

SILC, a hand-held cataract fragmentation device

Manual small-incision cataract surgery (MSICS) carries much of the world's cataract volume. SILC, the Small Incision Lenticular Cutter, breaks up and removes the cataract through an incision of about 3 mm. It uses no ultrasound energy and no electricity, so it fits the existing MSICS workflow and its cost.

  • Patent WO2022241322 / EP4337150, first inventor, assigned to Johns Hopkins University

Preeclampsia triage for low-resource settings

Preeclampsia is a leading cause of maternal and newborn deaths, and it is often missed where blood-pressure screening is scarce. This device is designed for preeclampsia triage in low-resource settings, with an initial focus on Nairobi.

  • Gates Foundation Grand Challenges grant, as principal investigator
